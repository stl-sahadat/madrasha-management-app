# Module 03: Teachers & Staff (শিক্ষক ও কর্মচারী ব্যবস্থাপনা)

## Overview
Manages madrasa faculty, educational designations, monthly salary sheets, and daily payment disbursements.

## Features
- **Faculty Directory**: Records teacher qualifications, subjects taught, contact numbers, and join dates.
- **Salary Sheet Generator**: Automatic monthly salary sheet with deductions, advances, and net payable.
- **Daily Payment Breakdown**: Tracks daily salary advances and cash withdrawals.
