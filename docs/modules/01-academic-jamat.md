# Module 01: Academic & Jamat Management (জামাত ও একাডেমিক ব্যবস্থাপনা)

## Overview
Manages the madrasa academic structure across Islamic curriculum stages, class sections, and enrollment quotas.

## Supported Jamats (শ্রেণীসমূহ)
1. **হিফজ বিভাগ (Hifz Section)** — Quran memorization program.
2. **নাজেরা বিভাগ (Nazera Section)** — Quran recitation foundation.
3. **মক্তব ও কায়দা (Maktab & Qaidah)** — Early childhood foundational Noorani reading.
4. **কিতাব বিভাগ (Kitab Section)** — Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh), Arabic grammar, Hadith, Tafsir.
5. **ইবতিদাইয়্যাহ ১ম-৫ম (Ebtedayee 1st to 5th)** — Primary curriculum combining Islamic and general studies.

## Core Capabilities
- Jamat capacity monitoring and live student counts.
- Dynamic fee structures per Jamat (tuition, boarding/khuraki).
- Jamat-based filtering across all 14 reports, SMS broadcasts, and ID card batches.
