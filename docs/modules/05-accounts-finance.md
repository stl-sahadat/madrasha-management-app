# Module 05: Accounts & Financial Ledger (হিসাব ও ভাউচার ব্যবস্থাপনা)

## Overview
Full double-entry-style ledger with 0.01 Paisa currency precision and Bengali in-words verbalizer.

## Components & Routes
- `Deposit Vouchers` — Fee collection, donations, zakat inflows.
- `Expense Vouchers` — Teacher salaries, bazar expenses, utilities, maintenance.
- `Account Creation & List` — Cash, bKash, Nagad, Bank accounts.
- `Balance Transfer` — Inter-account transfer with validation and rollback safety.
- `Balancesheet & Cash Ledger` — Real-time opening, current, and closing balances.
