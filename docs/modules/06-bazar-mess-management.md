# Module 06: Bazar & Mess Management (দৈনিক ও মাসিক বাজার খরচ)

## Overview
Comprehensive mess accounting for residential madrasa dining and student meals.

## Features
- Daily bazar entry with itemized purchase breakdown.
- Monthly bazar summaries and meal cost division.
- Comparison with collected Khuraki fees to calculate kitchen surplus or deficit.
