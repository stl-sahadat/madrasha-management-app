# Module 08: SMS Gateway & Broadcast (এসএমএস ব্যবস্থাপনা)

## Overview
Automated and manual SMS broadcast engine tailored for Bangladeshi telecom standards.

## Technical Specifications
- **English GSM-7 Formula**: 160 characters for part 1, 153 characters for concatenated parts.
- **Bengali UCS-2 Formula**: 70 characters for part 1, 67 characters for concatenated parts. The formula includes the `+91` character jump offset when Bengali characters are introduced.
- **Broadcast Targeting**:
  - All students / Specific Jamat
  - Fee dues alert
  - Absent notification
  - Teachers & Staff notice
